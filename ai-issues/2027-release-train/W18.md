The practice hub offers a short goal chooser, while the guide retains its explanatory
role and offers the same entry point. Five choices configure actual existing tasks:
first characters, static recognition, words, listening, and typing. The first set
reuses the guide's E T A N I M S O sequence. Review is absent until W20 implements
a real queue. No new route, account, adaptive claim, or raw URL payload.
The one-use module-memory handoff carries a known goal ID, expires after five minutes,
and works without storage. Reload uses the exercise's normal saved preferences.

Practice pool, mode, and best streak restore atomically to avoid stale per-pool
settings overriding an explicit session. Manual overrides still persist. Existing
storage keys remain; starter receives the same registered policies as existing pools.
Typing preferences now restore after hydration, preventing divergent SSR output and
premature preference writes. Listening does not autoplay. Existing direct controls,
audio engine/settings, algorithms, answer semantics, and scores remain intact.
